About us

Founded June 2023 by three design engineers from the workholding industry.

Our story

Instinctive Technologies Private Limited was founded in June 2023 by three design engineers, each with 7 to 12+ years in design, manufacturing and prove-out of fixtures. We started with fixtures and we keep our designs simple: good quality, at the lowest workable cost, delivered in a short lead time.

Our approach is cost, quality, delivery, in that order of conversation with every customer.

Management team
